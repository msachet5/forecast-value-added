Method

Demand classes use the Syntetos-Boylan cut-offs (ADI 1.32, CV² 0.49) on history before the first forecast. ABC uses cumulative value share (80/95); XYZ uses the coefficient of variation of period demand (0.5/1.0). WAPE = sum |forecast - actual| / sum actual. Bias = sum (forecast - actual) / sum actual, positive = over-forecast. MASE scales MAE by the in-sample seasonal naive MAE (season length 52). FVA = previous step's error minus this step's error; positive adds value.
